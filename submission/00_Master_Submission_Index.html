Autonomous Monocular 3D Gaussian Splatting Digital Twin & Indoor Spatial Navigation Engine
Challenge: NammaSpace (Juspay)
Round: 1 (Reconstruction & Roaming)
Status: Production Ready (60 FPS)
Date: October 2026

Executive Overview: NammaSpace delivers an end-to-end framework transforming monocular smartphone video into an ultra-high-fidelity 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) digital twin running universally in standard web browsers at locked 60+ FPS. Evaluators can explore three complete architectural environments encompassing 6,000,000+ splat primitives, with Google Maps-style indoor landmark pins, real-time spatial search, and obstacle-aware A* pathfinding.

✨ Key Architectural Features & Evaluation Highlights
🏛️ Triple-Environment 6.0M+ Showcase
Instant switching across Ultra-HD Studio (1.13M), Commercial Atrium (1.73M), and Spatial Courtyard (3.14M). All mathematically leveled to ground plane Y=0.
📍 Google Maps Indoor 3D Pins & Search
Dynamic 3D billboard pin markers with pulsing floor target halos and instant search bar filtering that illuminates matching world pins with keyboard Enter navigation.
🧭 Obstacle-Aware A* Waypoint Pathfinding
Projects an animated glowing dashed walking path on the floor avoiding obstacles, with real-time distance and estimated walking time calculations.
📡 Holographic 2D Tactical Floor Radar
Real-time synchronized canvas minimap showing player position, orientation delta chevron, dynamic 60° vision cone, and active waypoint blips.
