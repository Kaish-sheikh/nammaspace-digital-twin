Abstract

Traditional indoor 3D digital twins often require expensive LiDAR scanners, photogrammetry rigs, or synthetic CAD modeling that fails to replicate real-world material reflectance and complex lighting. In this work, we present NammaSpace, an end-to-end framework that transforms video capture into an interactive, photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) digital twin running entirely in standard web browsers at 60+ FPS without local installation. By implementing a standardized 4-pass orbital video capture protocol, our pipeline extracts dense frames with >75% inter-frame visual overlap, resolving previous sparse triangulation failures. Using headless COLMAP Structure-from-Motion (SfM) and GPU-accelerated rasterization on NVIDIA hardware, our system delivers a dual-twin spatial architecture: a flagship Ultra-HD spatial studio twin with 1,130,000+ photorealistic Gaussian splats loaded by default, complemented by an expansive outdoor campus courtyard twin with 3,135,000+ photorealistic splats. Furthermore, we front-load Round 2 spatial intelligence requirements into our WebGL runtime, featuring interactive 3D Points of Interest (POIs) with raycasting interaction, obstacle-aware Catmull-Rom spline pathfinding, and a real-time synchronized 2D floor radar.

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

The proliferation of digital twin applications—spanning real estate inspection, architectural heritage conservation, and indoor navigation—demands high visual fidelity, low capture cost, and seamless browser-based distribution. Previous neural radiance field (NeRF) representations suffer from slow volume rendering rendering them impractical for edge consumer devices. While 3D Gaussian Splatting provides real-time rasterization, naive smartphone capture typically results in severe anisotropic artifacts ("needle splats") and sparse tracking failure due to low visual overlap and non-static illumination.

Our project addresses these limitations through three core contributions:

  1. Standardized Capture Protocol (SOP): A rigorous 4-pass trajectory guaranteeing >75% spatial overlap, eliminating floaters and incomplete reconstruction.
  2. High-Performance Dual-Twin Architecture: CUDA-accelerated optimization delivering both an indoor architectural studio twin (1.13M splats) and an expansive outdoor botanical courtyard twin (3.14M splats), running at locked 60+ FPS.
  3. Zero-Install WebGL Spatial Intelligence Engine: A Three.js viewer embedding interactive 3D pins, waypoint pathfinding with smooth Catmull-Rom splines, and a synchronized 2D floor radar.

2. Capture Protocol & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To prevent tracking loss during feature matching, we developed and validated a 4-pass orbital trajectory:

  • Pass 1 (Eye-Level Orbit, 0° Pitch): Establishes the primary horizontal perimeter baseline (~1.6 m height).
  • Pass 2 (Low-Angle Furniture Orbit, +25° Pitch): Captures floor geometry, chair legs, and baseboards (~1.0 m height), eliminating ground plane floating.
  • Pass 3 (High-Angle Ceiling Orbit, -25° Pitch): Establishes room height limits, ceiling light fixtures, and wall fixtures (~1.8 m height).
  • Pass 4 (Center Inward Pan & POI Sweeps): Dense 360° rotational pass focusing on primary interaction zones (workstation, display, doorway).

Optical Conditioning

  • Auto-Exposure / Auto-Focus Lock (AE/AF Lock): Pinned at a median-lux zone to prevent fluctuating brightness.
  • Diffused Illumination: Controlled ambient lighting without variable shadows or moving dynamic objects.
  • Temporal Extraction Rate: Dense frame extraction delivering high camera registration density, completely resolving sparse anisotropic needle artifacts.

3. Photogrammetry & 3D Gaussian Splatting Optimization

The dense frame collection is processed via headless COLMAP using the SIMPLE_RADIAL camera model to accommodate subtle radial lens distortions without over-parameterization.

Mathematical Formulation of 3DGS

Each 3D Gaussian is parameterized by its mean center \(\boldsymbol{\mu} \in \mathbb{R}^3\), full 3D covariance matrix \(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\), opacity \(\alpha \in [0, 1]\), and color represented via spherical harmonics (SH) coefficients:

\[\boldsymbol{\Sigma} = \mathbf{R} \mathbf{S} \mathbf{S}^T \mathbf{R}^T\]

where \(\mathbf{S} = \text{diag}(s_x, s_y, s_z)\) represents anisotropic scaling factors and \(\mathbf{R}\) is the rotation matrix derived from unit quaternions \(\mathbf{q}\). During tile-based rasterization, the color \(C\) of a screen pixel \(\mathbf{p}\) is computed via \(\alpha\)-blending ordered Gaussians:

\[C(\mathbf{p}) = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{N}} c_i \alpha_i \prod_{j=1}^{i-1} (1 - \alpha_j)\]

Training & Convergence

  • Steps: CUDA-accelerated optimization iterations on an NVIDIA Tesla GPU.
  • Loss Function: Combined photometric \(\mathcal{L}_1\) loss and structural similarity \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{SSIM}}\):
    \[\mathcal{L} = (1 - \lambda)\mathcal{L}_1 + \lambda(1 - \text{SSIM}) \quad \text{with } \lambda = 0.2\]
  • Final Metrics: High structural convergence error of <0.67 pixels across camera viewpoints, ensuring crisp object boundaries.

4. WebGL Runtime Architecture & Spatial Navigation

The client architecture is engineered for immediate zero-install execution in any WebGL-compliant browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

Key Interactive Features

  1. 3D Interactive POI Markers: Visualized using dual-mesh rendering: a core glowing sphere, an expanding vertical beacon, and an oscillating ground ring (THREE.RingGeometry). Accompanied by billboarded 2D canvas text sprites dynamically facing the camera. Screen-space mouse clicks are mapped into 3D world space using THREE.Raycaster.
  2. Obstacle-Aware Pathfinding & Spline Interpolation: Navigable space is modeled using an obstacle-free topological waypoint graph. Waypoints are routed through safe corridors and smoothed using a continuous 3D Catmull-Rom spline curve (THREE.CatmullRomCurve3). The route renders as an animated glowing neon dashed ribbon (THREE.LineDashedMaterial with dynamic dashOffset streaming). Arrival detection triggers automated glassmorphic alerts when player distance to destination is < 1.0 m.
  3. Synchronized 2D Floor Radar: A real-time HUD minimap projects the 3D player coordinates and heading onto a 2D floor plane with rotating delta fighter chevron, realistic FOV cone, metric range rings, and target navigation vectors.

6. Conclusion & Round 2 Roadmap

Our NammaSpace digital twin pipeline bridges the gap between raw video capture and high-performance interactive web distribution. By adhering to our validated SOP, dense frame extraction, and delivering both an indoor architectural studio twin (1.13M splats) and an expansive outdoor courtyard twin (3.14M splats), we achieve state-of-the-art fidelity and instant browser accessibility. Ahead of Round 2, our architecture already incorporates working POIs, waypoint navigation, and real-time floor radar. Future development will integrate automated semantic segmentation for dynamic room asset labeling and WebXR support for immersive mobile VR/AR walkthroughs.
