🎮 CONTROLS & ARCHITECTURE

NammaSpace converts handheld smartphone video into an ultra-low latency, browser-native 3D Gaussian Splatting scene.

  • Movement: Use W, A, S, D to walk.
  • Look Around: Move your mouse while in walk mode.
  • Dollhouse View: Press V or click the bottom switcher for an orbital aerial view.
  • Waypoints: Click any landmark pin in the 3D room or select from the left dock to generate an obstacle-avoiding floor path.
  • Floor Radar: Top-right minimap tracks your real-time heading and target vectors.
